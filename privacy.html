Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

OpenX (openx-mo.vercel.app) is operated by Mountain Grey Design LTD. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. If you have any questions, contact us at glossmic@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

  • Email address — provided at checkout so we can deliver your Pro licence key and send purchase-related messages.
  • Purchase information — order details and licence status, handled through our payment processor Paddle (see section 3).
  • Messages you send us — for example emails asking for support.

OpenX tools do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, address or phone number to use the free tools.

2. What we do not collect

We never see or store your card or bank details — all payments are processed by Paddle. We do not run advertising trackers on this site, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. How your information is shared

Your purchase is processed by Paddle, who acts as the merchant of record (reseller) for your order. Paddle collects the payment and billing information needed to complete the sale and handles sales tax, invoicing and refunds. Paddle's own privacy policy applies to the information it collects. Apart from Paddle, we do not share your personal information with any third party, unless required by law.

4. Local storage

OpenX tools store your work, settings and licence status in your own browser's local storage, on your device. This data stays with you and is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data will remove it. We do not operate a cloud account system and we cannot recover work stored only in your browser.

5. How long we keep information

We keep purchase records for as long as needed to provide your licence, handle refunds and meet our legal and accounting obligations. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your enquiry.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it (where we are not legally required to keep it). Email glossmic@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above will change, and significant changes will be noted on this page.
