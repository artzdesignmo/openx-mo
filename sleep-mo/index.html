About

Sleep Mo is a planning toy built on the 90-minute sleep-cycle model. Times are estimates — real sleep varies night to night. General sleep information only, not medical advice. If you snore heavily, stop breathing at night, or feel exhausted despite enough sleep, talk to a doctor. Everything is stored in this browser only: no account, no cloud, no tracking.
