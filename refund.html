Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. 30-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with OpenX Pro, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of your purchase. No questions asked.

2. How to request a refund

Email us with the email address you used at checkout and, if you have it, your order number. We will confirm your request and arrange the refund through Paddle.

3. How refunds are paid

Refunds are issued by Paddle to your original payment method. Once approved, the refund is usually processed within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider. Your Pro licence will be deactivated when a refund is issued.

4. After 30 days

After the 30-day period, purchases are generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable consumer protection law.

5. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights. Buyers in the European Union and the United Kingdom have a 14-day cooling-off right for distance purchases, as provided by law.

6. Subscriptions

If you cancel a Monthly subscription, Pro stays active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. The current month is not refunded on cancellation alone — use the 30-day guarantee above if you want a refund instead.
