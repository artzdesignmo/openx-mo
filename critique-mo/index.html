How the scores work

Every score is pixel maths, computed on a 240×240 copy of your image — no AI, no server, no taste judgement. Contrast is a WCAG-style luminance ratio; Harmony reads hue relationships on the colour wheel; Balance weighs where the visual mass sits; Whitespace measures quiet area; Focus measures how strongly one area pulls the eye. A high score means the pixels are balanced. It cannot judge meaning, brand fit, or originality — that part is still yours.
